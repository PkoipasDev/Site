Multiplions !

Times tables, through play

Multiplions ! helps children learn and revise the times tables from 1 to 10, at their own pace. The app spots the multiplications that are hardest to remember and offers them more often, until they are mastered.

  • No account
  • No ads
  • Works offline

Features

Practise

Free practice, timed assessment, race against the clock, and games: Find the odd one out, Maze, Balloons, Expert challenge.

Play together

Two players on the same screen, with four duel modes, including a cooperative mode.

Understand

Lessons, tips and advice to remember more easily.

Revise away from the screen

Oral test led by an adult, exercise sheets and cards to print.

Track progress

Mastery level for each multiplication, progress charts and practice reminders.
