Privacy policy – Multiplions !

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Multiplions ! is an Android app for children to practise their times tables. It is published by Benoît MALLET, sole proprietor (entrepreneur individuel, EI), under the trade name PkoiPas. The app is designed to collect no personal data.

What the app does not do

  • No account or password.
  • No advertising.
  • No usage statistics, audience measurement or trackers.
  • No data is sent to any server, neither to the publisher nor to a third party.

Data stored on the device

Profiles (first name or nickname, results, settings, reminders) are stored only in the app’s storage, on the device. The publisher has no access to them.

This data only leaves the device if you export a file: you then choose where to save it or who to send it to. An exported file contains the first names, the results with the date of each attempt, the settings and a randomly generated installation identifier, used only to merge results from several devices.

Permissions requested

  • Notifications: to show the practice reminders you schedule.
  • Exact alarms: to trigger these reminders at the chosen time.
  • Storage (older Android versions only): to save the backups and PDFs you export in the Documents folder.

The app does not request the camera, microphone, contacts or location.

Reminders and notifications

A reminder is a notification scheduled on the phone, without going through a server. Its text contains the profile’s first name: depending on the phone’s settings, it may appear on the lock screen. You can turn off a reminder in “My profile” or in the phone’s settings.

PDF files

The written test and the printable cards are created on the device, without the internet. A PDF is only shared if you choose to share or print it.

Phone backup

The app’s data is excluded from the phone’s automatic backup. To switch devices, export a backup (Settings, Backup), then import it on the new device.

Google services

The app is downloaded and updated through Google Play, which applies its own privacy rules. The publisher only receives aggregate statistics from Google (number of installs, countries, crashes), which cannot be used to identify you.

Children

The app is intended for children, under the responsibility of an adult who creates the profiles. It does not ask the child for any personal information. We recommend using a first name or a nickname, without a surname.

Security

Data stays in the app’s private space, protected by Android, and never travels over the internet. An exported file is not encrypted: keep it as you would a personal document.

Retention and deletion

Data stays on the device until you delete it. You can delete a profile in “My profile”, or erase everything by clearing the app’s data in the phone’s settings, or by uninstalling it. A recovery copy of an unreadable backup is kept on the device for 30 days, then deleted.

The publisher holds no copy: it cannot view, recover or delete your data on your behalf. Remember to export a backup if you want to keep it.

If you write to the publisher

If you write to support@pkoipas.fr, your email address and message are used only to reply to you. They are neither sold nor passed on to third parties, and are deleted no later than 1 year after the last exchange.

You can ask to access, correct or delete them at the same address. You may also lodge a complaint with the CNIL, the French data protection authority.

Changes

This policy may change as the app evolves. The date of the last update is shown at the top of the page. The same policy is available in the app (Information, Privacy policy).
